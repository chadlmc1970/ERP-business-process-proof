# Research brief (shared by all research lines)

Experiment: treat one common business process as a conjecture ("to get outcome X under constraints Y, you must do A, B, C this way") and try to disprove it. Discovery, not confirmation. It is fine, and useful, to conclude the conventional process survives.

Selected process: the three-way match in accounts payable (purchase order, goods receipt, supplier invoice compared before payment).

Frame: US public mid/large company, SOX, US GAAP, external audit. Non-US rules only where they change a conclusion.

Rules: vendor neutral, no product pitches, no commercial roadmap. Primary and authoritative sources first (statutes, PCAOB/SEC/COSO/AICPA, FAR, peer-reviewed work, original surveys). Vendor blogs are weak evidence: label them, never present them as benchmarks. Never invent a figure. Tag each claim: Established / Supported hypothesis / Speculative / Rejected. Say plainly what could not be verified. Do not use em dashes.
